Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Plotnikov Yevhenii Oleksandrovych FOP ("we", "us", or "our"), a registered individual entrepreneur under the laws of Ukraine, collects, uses, and discloses your information when you use our mobile applications distributed via the Google Play Store and Apple App Store.

1. Information Collection and Use

We believe in user privacy and minimize data collection wherever possible. Depending on how you interact with our applications, the following data processing rules apply:

  • Financial & Billing Information: Our applications support in-app purchases. All transactions are securely processed externally via the Google Play Billing and/or Apple In-App Purchase systems. We never directly collect, access, or store your credit card numbers, bank account details, or billing credentials.
  • Device & Diagnostic Data: We may collect anonymous technical information, such as your device model, operating system version, and system performance logs (crash data) purely to diagnose software issues and optimize app performance.

2. Third-Party Services

We do not share, sell, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, our applications rely on trusted external service providers that process data independently in accordance with their own privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services & Google Play Billing: Used for app distribution, account authentication, and secure transaction infrastructure. Google Privacy Policy
  • Apple App Store & In-App Purchases: Used for iOS app distribution and payment handling. Apple Privacy Policy

3. Data Retention and Security

Any technical metadata collected via crashes is kept strictly for the duration required to resolve the bug and is stored securely. Since financial information is routed entirely through Google and Apple infrastructure, your payment security is backed by native ecosystem-level protection frameworks.

4. Children's Privacy

Our mobile applications do not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions complying with GDPR). If we discover that a child has inadvertently provided us with personal data, we will immediately delete it from our development and diagnostic records.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to update or modify our Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will become immediately effective upon posting the revised document on this URL. We encourage you to review this page periodically to stay informed about our data management workflows.
